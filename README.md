# Sahaay

Open-source emergency help for Indian cities. Starting with Hyderabad.

One app for the minutes that matter: who to call, what to do while you wait, and keeping your people in the loop. No middlemen, no paid ranking. Every call goes straight to 108, 112, the police, or the ambulance provider you choose.

## What works today
- SOS home with five emergencies: road accident, medical, fire, danger, woman in danger
- Road accident mode: golden-hour clock, triage → a script to read to 108, jobs (ambulance, police, road clearance, hospital, family) with owners and status, photos, after-accident checklist, timeline
- Ambulance options: 108, RED.Health, Apollo 1066, CARE, with cost, life support and destination. Tracks the one you called, reads the booking SMS for vehicle number and ETA, reminds you to cancel the second ambulance, and remembers whether each provider answered
- Medical: symptom picker, CPR with an audible 110/min beat and a 2-minute swap timer
- Danger and woman in danger: silent location share, fake incoming call, hold-to-sound alarm, safe places nearby
- Hospitals sorted by distance; Free (govt), PM-RAHAT cashless, private, children
- First aid for 9 situations; Good Samaritan protection explained
- Medical ID and emergency contacts, stored only on the phone
- Installable (PWA), works offline

## Not yet
- Sharing an emergency between phones (live accident page, merging duplicate reports, nearby helpers)
- Verified hospital capability data and PM-RAHAT enrolment
- Telugu and Hindi
- Integrations with 108/112 for real dispatch status

## Data notes
Provider numbers come from each provider's public pages. Hospital positions are approximate. Nothing here is a dispatch service: if in doubt, call 112.
